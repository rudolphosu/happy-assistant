Enroll in Household Reminder Service

Operated by Nolan Rudolph, sole proprietor

Household Reminder Service is a private, non-commercial reminder application for the members of a single household. It sends a daily summary of household chores, home maintenance, birthdays, and personal deadlines that members create themselves, plus confirmations when a member texts in to add or update a reminder. Enrollment is by invitation: household members are sent a link to this page.

How enrollment works

  1. A household member receives the link to this page.
  2. The member texts the keyword START (or JOIN) from their own mobile phone to +1 (312) 847-3791.
  3. The service immediately replies with an enrollment confirmation and the disclosures shown below.
  4. No messages are sent to any number that has not texted START.
  5. The member can reply STOP at any time to opt out, and receives one confirmation.
