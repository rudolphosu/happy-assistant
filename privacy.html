Privacy Policy

Household Reminder Service · Last updated: 2026-07-26

This service is a private, non-commercial SMS/MMS reminder system operated by Nolan Rudolph as a sole proprietor. It is used by exactly two people: the operator and their spouse. It is not offered to the public, and there is no sign-up.

Information collected

  • The mobile phone numbers of the two participants.
  • The content of messages sent to and from the service.
  • Reminder and task data that participants create (titles, schedules, notes).

How it is used

Solely to operate the reminder service — interpreting incoming messages, storing reminders, and sending scheduled reminder messages back to the two participants. It is used for no other purpose.

Service providers

Message data passes through two providers, each acting only to operate this service:

  • Twilio Inc. — message delivery. See Twilio's privacy policy.
  • Anthropic PBC — interprets message text so reminders can be created from plain language. See Anthropic's privacy policy.

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in the United States.

What is never done

  • Information is never sold, rented, or licensed.
  • Information is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • No advertising or promotional messages are sent.
  • No mobile information is shared with third parties for their own independent use.

Retention

Message transcripts are automatically deleted after 30 days. Reminder data is retained until a participant deletes it. All data is deleted if the service is shut down.

Opting out

Either participant may reply STOP to any message to stop all messages immediately. Reply HELP for assistance.
