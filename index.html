Household Reminder Service

A private, non-commercial SMS/MMS reminder service operated by Nolan Rudolph as a sole proprietor. Two participants create reminders for household chores, recurring maintenance, birthdays, and personal deadlines by text message, and receive a daily summary of what is due.

The service is not offered to the public. Household members enroll by invitation, by texting the keyword START to the service number from their own phone; nothing is sent to any number that has not done so. No promotional or marketing messages are sent.

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe, or HELP for assistance.
